details: Unique just in terms of scale! Due to the price of renting period costumes, most of the pieces were hand constructed, and so implementation was very labor intensive.
responsibilities:
  - Developed costume concepts from script analysis, period and cultural clothing research, and the director’s vision for tone, era and color palette
  - Coordinated with the scenic and lighting designers to keep the production’s look unified
  - Produced design documents including an artistic concept, image research, character breakdowns and a design preview presentation through iterative meetings with creative team
  - Sourced costumes on a budget from thrift and vintage stores, online retailers and constructing from scratch
  - Recruited and trained a run crew, set call times with stage management, and handled quick changes and notes during tech week
