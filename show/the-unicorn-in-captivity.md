description: Student written play by Ursula Sturgeon -- Marcie Greene, spinster at large, absolutely NEEDS to be married- especially now. The man who’s promised himself to her (at least, according to her) has gotten engaged with someone else! Desperate to finally have that marriage certificate in hand, she goes to domestic attorney Bert Toller’s office for help- only to discover that he’s quite busy with the Almavivas, a young couple “turbodivorcing” each other, and Angel, a hyperactive lesbian coffee girl. Marcie is left to juggle the threads so she can get the man she wants- only to start wondering if there’s perhaps something more about herself she’s been pushing down all this time.
