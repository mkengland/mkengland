It follows classic characters, all pursuing a particular objective, that brings all of them together in the woods. The baker and his wife want to have a child; Cinderella, to attend the King’s festival; and lastly, Jack, who wishes his cow could give milk. Everyone’s wish comes true, but the consequences of their actions prove to be costly.
