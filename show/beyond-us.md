description: Beyond Us takes place in the small town of Amherst, New Hampshire. The story follows Barry Oltrenoi, a young college student working himself numb on his Doctorate in Education while managing a chaotic but loving relationship with his best friend,Richard (Rich) Defranco, a suave man who lives for women, partying, and looking after Barry. In an attempt to get Barry out of a rut, Rich takes him to a party where Barry meets Elizabeth, the woman of his dreams. Barry quickly falls in love and goes to every extent to find her. We follow Barry as he rereads a series of letters left to him by Rich as a gift, showing Barry and Rich’s lives through the late ’60s and early ’70s, a time they both hold close to their hearts.

details: 
  - Drafted and created original pieces for most lead characters
responsibilities:
  - Developed costume concepts from script analysis, period and cultural clothing research, and the director’s vision for tone, era and color palette
  - Coordinated with the scenic and lighting designers to keep the production’s look unified
  - Produced design documents including an artistic concept, image research, character breakdowns and a design preview presentation through iterative meetings with creative team
  - Sourced costumes on a budget from thrift and vintage stores, online retailers and constructing from scratch
  - Recruited and trained a run crew, set call times with stage management, and handled quick changes and notes during tech week
